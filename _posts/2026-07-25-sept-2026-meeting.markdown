We'll be at the [Sulzer Regional Library](https://maps.app.goo.gl/s9BvHjHzaQwGrprc9) again on **9/8/26 from 6-7:30pm** to meet up, talk shop, and see a solid talk hot on the heels of DEF CON 34. Bring your recently acquired badgelife and con swag to show off, and make some new friends! 

We'll have an updated version of **Bullying Your Co-Workers with Phishing Emails** by Mr. Sprawl who presented this year at BSides312. We probably have time for one more talk; if anyone has something they're up to share drop it in #speakers on Discord. 

The area around the library has several spots to grab food after the meeting including Small Cheval, Lou Malnatis, Jimmy's Pizza Cafe, Taqueria El Asadero, and several other solid spots.

The area is accessible via:

- CTA Brown Train Line - Western station
- No. 49 Western Ave. & 49B North Western Ave
- No. 50 Damen Ave.
- No. 78 Montrose
- No. 81 Lawrence (connects to red line near Broadway)

Parking: The library has a free parking lot behind the library, enter on Sunnyside Ave. There is also free street parking on some of the side streets (please read signs) and city lots are just north on Lincoln as well.
