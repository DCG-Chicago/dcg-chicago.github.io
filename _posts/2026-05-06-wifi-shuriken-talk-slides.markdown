We had a great talk at the May meeting from CoD_Segfault about their WiFi Shuriken project. Below is a rundown for anyone who missed it.

## WiFi Shuriken

CoD_Segfault walked us through their WiFi Shuriken project — a custom wardriving hardware build — along with a deep dive into the history of wardriving. They covered everything from the early days of wardriving to modern microcontroller-based approaches and what it takes to go from a prototype to shipping hardware.

Slides from the talk are available on [their GitHub](https://github.com/CoD-Segfault/talk-slides).
